Built from the marked-up screenshots of the current system and the wnShipping USA Manifest Format (July 2024) spec. Everything circled has been kept, everything crossed out has gone.

1. Click Load demo data below (40 parcels, 2 containers, MAWB 695-11090645) — or drop in one of your own manifest CSVs.
2. Go to the Scan tab and trigger the NETUM scanner at a real parcel label.
3. Tell us what happens — that single test answers our biggest open question.

Nothing is saved to a server: this demo lives only in your browser and holds no real customer data.
